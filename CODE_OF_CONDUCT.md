# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in SELMA Labs a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Positive participation includes:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from the
  experience; and
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission,
and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents
the project in public. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that conflict with this code and may issue a correction, warning, temporary
ban, or permanent ban based on the impact and pattern of the behavior.

For an enforcement concern, contact the repository owner through a private
GitHub channel when one is available. If no private channel is available, open
a minimal issue requesting private contact and do not include sensitive details
or information about affected people. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
fairly as practical, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
