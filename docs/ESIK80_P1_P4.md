## Creative decisions

- Working title changed from `Crimson Silence` to `ESIK//80` (`THRESHOLD//80`)
  after a basic collision scan found several existing entertainment uses of the
  former title. This is not trademark clearance.
- Akira Arel is the anime protagonist. The game uses a separate player-created
  relay operative so game choices can matter without breaking the anime canon.
- FHD-80 remains the central hook. It causes recognition failure, not physical
  face distortion and not zombie behavior.
- Mina has congenital prosopagnosia but is not immune. Her adaptations make her
  valuable without turning disability into a superpower.
- Noe Varga is a credible institutional antagonist, not the inventor of evil for
  its own sake. His conflict with the heroes is consent versus continuity.
- The season resolves the truth-disclosure plot while leaving the external
  outbreak, Akira's illness and district outcomes available for later stories.

## Medical grounding boundary

FHD-80, MAB-TJ, ADEX-R and LIMEN are fictional. The olfactory route is used
as a plausible genre foundation because experimental neurotropic-virus research
has documented spread from olfactory tissue toward the brain. This does not make
the precise FHD-80 timetable or its treatments medically real. Prosopagnosia is
represented as loss of facial familiarity rather than loss of normal eyesight.
